# Future features (not scheduled)

## اسأل المسرّب (Ask Mosarib), discussed 2026-11-03
No AI: offline rules and the 032 slot engine (person, period, operation type, question kind) matched
to a fixed catalogue of about 40 questions. The answer is a card: the number with تفقيط, plus open,
share and read-aloud buttons. Questions can be spoken (030 sheet).
- **Balances and people:** كم على فلان، آخر سقي/سداد لفلان، أكثر المديونين، مين ما سدد من شهر،
  ساعات فلان هذا الشهر.
- **Numbers and reports:** دخل/ساعات اليوم، قبض امس، صرف الديزل هذا الشهر، ربح الشهر، مقارنة بالشهر
  الماضي، شراء الساعات والباقي لصاحب البئر. "كشف حساب فلان من اول الشهر" opens the statement
  prefilled (PDF / WhatsApp).
- **Specific operations:** voucher number, a session by name + day, amounts above X.
- **How-to:** about 20 short answers, each with a button that opens the screen.
- **Not understood:** "هل تقصد…" with the 3 closest questions, suggestion chips, and a local-only log of
  unanswered questions.
- **Limits:** no open analysis ("ليش ارباحي نازلة") and only common compound questions. AI could be
  an optional extra later.
- **Plan:**
  - v1: a bar at the top of Home with a mic; ~15 data questions and ~20 how-to answers.
  - v2: reports and periods, comparisons, finding an operation, growing from the log.
- **Open decisions:** free or subscriber-only parts; placement (Home bar recommended).
