## Decisions and doubts
- In `Formatters.kt`, `Locale.forLanguageTag("ar-u-nu-latn")` combined with `DecimalFormat("0", DecimalFormatSymbols(Locale.US))` was used for `SimpleDateFormat` instances (`formatDateFull`, `formatTime`, `formatDateTime`). This cleanly outputs Western Arabic numerals (0-9) for all numbers while retaining full Arabic names for days of the week, months, and morning/afternoon markers (`ص` / `م`).
- Semantic mapping for financial debt/credit was mapped directly to `MaterialTheme.mosarib.debt` (red tone) and `MaterialTheme.mosarib.credit` (green tone), which automatically resolve to dark-safe variants (`#F87171` / `#4ADE80`) in dark mode and high-contrast equivalents in light mode.
- `CustomersFilterRow.kt` was intentionally not touched, as specified in the task instructions (it is handled by Task 016).
