# Report 056: Well-owner purchase form like the irrigation form

## Summary
Rebuilt the body of `WellOwnerPurchaseBottomSheet` in the irrigation form's compact layout. Calculations, snapshot, save values, the 048 confirmation sheet, voice filling and guide targets are unchanged.

New order of elements:
1. Owner: `CustomerPickerField` (well owners only).
2. «من» / «إلى» boxes (tap time, tap date), then the row «المدة: [ساعات] [دقائق]» using `CompactDurationField`; a small overnight notice line when the end is on the next day.
3. One row: «سعر الساعة» | «الواصل (المدفوع الآن)», each with its تفقيط.
4. Card, two columns: «المستحق له» | «المتبقي بذمتك» (red with تفقيط when > 0, green «خالص» at 0).
5. «خيارات إضافية» (collapsed, auto-open when waste or notes are filled): waste hours/minutes (compact), net duration and waste credit line, notes.

Tabs removed. Editing the duration switches the internal mode to MANUAL, so «إلى» = «من» + duration. Editing «إلى» (time or date) adopts the shown end and switches back to PICKER mode.
