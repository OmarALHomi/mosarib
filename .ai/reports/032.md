# Report 032: Understand short and free-form spoken phrases (word order does not matter)

## Summary
Re-architected the voice command processing pipeline into a pure, slot-based parser independent of word order. Added data-driven vocabulary tables in `VoicePhraseRules.kt`, local-only unparsed phrase ring-buffer in `VoiceUnparsedLog.kt`, time-range and duration conflict warnings in `VoiceCommand.kt` and `VoiceUiComponents.kt`, and extended safe pre-fill across `HomeScreen.kt`, `SessionDialogs.kt`, and `VouchersScreen.kt`. Comprehensive unit testing with `VoicePhraseTableTest.kt` verifies 56 distinct test scenarios plus over 200 combinatorial variations across all common spoken patterns.

Following Review Round 1, fixed two edge cases:
1. "بالليل" with hours 1–4 resolves to 01:00–04:00 (after midnight) rather than PM.
2. Token-bounded candidate matching for paid and rate markers guarantees subsequent notes starting with "و" (such as "وقال الباقي بكره" or "وباقي عليه") never swallow or cancel paid amounts.

## Decisions and doubts
- **Alif Maqsura normalization parity**: `SpokenNumberParser.normalizeToken` maps all `'ى'` to `'ي'`, so words like `إلى` become `الي` and `حتى` become `حتي`. Time range and customer joiner regex patterns include both standard and normalized forms.
- **Unparsed phrase logging**: Stored strictly in local `SharedPreferences` (`app_settings` under key `voice_unparsed_log`) capped at 50 entries FIFO. No network access or transmission occurs.
- **Adjacent thousand grouping**: In phrases with adjacent numbers such as `10 الف 5 الف`, greedy number token grouping prevents combining multiple thousand tokens (`الف`) into a single candidate number, ensuring rate and paid amounts are resolved separately.
- **Midnight resolution**: Ambiguous hour resolution (`hour in 1..11`) excludes `0` (`نص الليل`) to prevent shifting midnight into noon.
- **Night hours 1–4**: Handled as after-midnight (01:00–04:00 AM) when qualified by "بالليل" / "الليل", while hours 6–12 remain PM.
- **Explicit payment priority**: Explicitly spoken payment amounts (e.g. `ودفع 5 الف`) take precedence over debt keywords, preserving both the payment and the remainder notes (e.g. `وباقي عليه`).
