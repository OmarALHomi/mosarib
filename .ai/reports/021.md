# Report 021: Reports screen redesign (filters, KPIs with words, trend, debtors, groups)

## Summary
Redesigned the reports screen to match the modern operations design system with unified `FilterDropdownChip` controls (period, group, account type, clear all). Added a 2×2 KPI grid featuring approximate amounts in Arabic words and previous-period percentage comparisons, a Canvas-based grouped-bar trend chart for revenue and collected cash, and new/enhanced cards for top debtors, group breakdown, pumping efficiency by pump, and well owner accounting. Removed deprecated `isAppDark()` and literal `sp` typography.

## Decisions and doubts
- **Adding `THIS_YEAR` to `OperationsFilterState.kt`**: When adding `ReportPeriod.THIS_YEAR` as instructed, `OperationsFilterState.kt:263` failed compilation because Kotlin requires `when (state.period)` to be exhaustive. We added the single missing branch `ReportPeriod.THIS_YEAR -> parts.add("هذه السنة")` so the project builds cleanly.
- **Debtors Count in Tile 4**: Tile 4 ("على العملاء الآن") shows the current receivable total and the subtitle "من N عميلاً", where N is the count of customers with positive receivable balance (`receivableBalance > 0.001`), with no previous-period comparison as specified.
- **Empty Trend Buckets**: When no transactions exist in the period, the trend card displays "لا توجد حركات في هذه الفترة". When transactions exist, empty buckets within the period are preserved with 0 amounts to maintain time axis scale.

## UI Descriptions (Light & Dark Themes)

### Light Theme
- **Filters Row (Top)**: Clean pill chips with subtle outline when default (`الفترة: الكل`, `المجموعة: الكل`, `نوع الحساب: الكل`), turning into filled teal container pills with bold labels when active. A soft red-tinted "مسح الكل ✕" chip appears at the end when any filter is active.
- **KPI Grid (2 × 2)**: Crisp white cards with 16dp rounded corners and soft drop shadows.
  - *Tile 1 (الإيراد)*: Muted label, bold amount in onSurface, approximate words in Arabic, and green comparison ("↑ 12% عن الفترة السابقة").
  - *Tile 2 (المحصّل نقداً)*: Primary teal amount, matching teal words, and comparison indicator.
  - *Tile 3 (صافي الربح)*: Emerald green amount when profit, dark red when deficit, with matching approximate words.
  - *Tile 4 (على العملاء الآن)*: Crimson debt amount, Arabic words, and muted subtitle "من 12 عميلاً".
- **Trend Chart**: Modern surface card with a header, legend pills (primary teal for revenue, emerald for collected), and 3 dashed horizontal Y-axis grid lines with compact values ("50k", "100k"). Grouped bars have rounded caps. Tapping a bar highlights the column with a soft primary overlay and displays a floating pill tooltip with the full date and formatted values.
- **Profit Distribution**: 3-color ratio bar (green for profit, purple-gray for well cost, red for pump expenses) with individual breakdown rows showing both numbers and words. Customer disbursements row is automatically hidden when 0.
- **Top Debtors (New)**: Subtle surface-variant list items with customer names, bold crimson debt numbers, and approximate words. Clicking a customer navigates to CustomerDetailScreen; clicking "عرض الكل" switches to Customers tab filtered by "عليه".
- **By Group (New)**: Group rows showing "إيراد · محصّل · آجل" in compact format (e.g. "50k"). Automatically hidden when fewer than 2 groups exist.
- **Pumping Efficiency**: Clean progress bar, big duration in Arabic ("454 ساعة"), and top 5 pump breakdown.
- **Well Owner Accounting**: Shows net purchases ("454 ساعة" instead of clock format), paid, and balance payable.

### Dark Theme
- **Filters Row**: Deep charcoal chips with outline border at default, high-contrast primaryContainer when selected.
- **KPI Grid & Cards**: Elevated dark gray surfaces (`colorScheme.surface`) with soft elevation glow. Muted texts use subtle cool gray (`mosarib.textMuted`), credit amounts glow in vibrant light emerald, and debt amounts use high-visibility coral red.
- **Trend Chart**: Dark background with low-opacity dashed grid lines (`outlineVariant` at 50% alpha). Primary bars and success bars maintain clear contrast against dark surfaces. Selected bar overlay uses 10% primary tint. Tooltip surface stands out with `surfaceVariant` background.
- **Top Debtors & Groups**: Items use translucent dark surfaceVariant containers with high readability.

## Round 2: Review Fixes (2026-11-02)

### Changes Made:
1. **Trend Chart RTL Direction & Alignment**:
   - Fixed the RTL mirroring bug by computing physical coordinates once and using Arabic reading order: **oldest on the right, newest on the left**.
   - `Canvas` and `drawText` use the identical `centerX` coordinate for both bar groups and X-axis labels, eliminating any layout mirroring mismatch.
   - Positioned Y-axis labels on the right edge of the chart card.
   - Display labels under every bar when $\le$ 8 buckets, and every second bar otherwise without overlap.
   - Tooltip and selection highlight aligned with the unified right-to-left slot calculation.
2. **Arabic Month Labels & Compact Numbers**:
   - Switched month labels to the full official Arabic names list (`يناير، فبراير، مارس، أبريل، مايو، يونيو، يوليو، أغسطس، سبتمبر، أكتوبر، نوفمبر، ديسمبر`), removing all shortened variants (`فبر/أبر/يون/أغس`).
   - Updated `ReportsFilter.formatCompactNumber` to output Arabic unit names (`2.3 مليون`, `771 ألف`) with 0-9 digits instead of `M`/`k`.
   - Updated and added unit tests in `ReportsFilterTest`.
3. **Pumping Efficiency Metric Separation**:
   - Put "إجمالي التشغيل" and "هدر المسرب" on separate, full-width rows with clear key-value alignment so they never touch or run together on narrow screens.
4. **Top Debtors Full-Width Layout**:
   - Redesigned each debtor row into a 2-line layout: Line 1 gives the debtor name full available width with the debt amount at the end, and Line 2 displays the approximate Arabic words underneath across the full card width.
