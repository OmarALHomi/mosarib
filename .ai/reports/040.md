# Report 040: Spotlight tours and first-time tips in every screen

## Summary
Implemented the interactive spotlight tour and first-time guidance architecture under `core/ui/guide/`. Replaced the static legacy dialog with dynamic spotlight overlays pointing directly to elements on screen with cut-outs and pulsing rings, persistent preferences in `mosarib_guides` (with automatic migration from `LicenseManager`), inline hint banners, a unified Help Center screen (`HelpScreen.kt`) with 8 Q&A cards and interactive navigation, and full integration into Home, Session Form, Voucher Form, Well-Owner Purchase Form, and the navigation bar.

## Tours & Guidance Implemented (Phase 1)
1. **Home (`home`)**:
   - «سقي جديد»: «سجّل دورة الري: العميل، المدة، والسعر.»
   - «إدخال صوتي»: «قل العملية بصوتك، مثل: مع أحمد ساعتين من 10 آلاف.»
   - «قبض وصرف»: «سندات القبض من العملاء، والصرف للمصروفات.»
   - «آخر دورات السقي»: «اضغط أي دورة لتعديلها أو مشاركتها.»
   - «التقرير الكامل»: «كشف شامل PDF لكل الحسابات.»
2. **Session Form (`session_form`)**:
   - Customer selection (`session_customer`), field mic (`session_mic`), time choice (`session_time_choice`), hourly price (`session_price`), and save button (`session_save`).
   - First-time inline hint above well-owner netting checkbox (`hint_session_well_owner`).
3. **Voucher Form (`voucher_form`)**:
   - Voucher type toggle (`voucher_type`), customer selection (`voucher_customer`), amount input with Arabic words (`voucher_amount`), and save button (`voucher_save`).
4. **Well-Owner Purchase Form (`purchase_form`)**:
   - Well owner picker (`purchase_owner`), hours and waste calculation card (`purchase_hours`), rate per hour (`purchase_price`), and save button (`purchase_save`).
5. **Navigation Bar (`nav`)**:
   - Sequential highlight across Operations, Customers, Reports, and Settings.
6. **Help Controls & Settings**:
   - Small «؟» restart button added to header bars of Home and every form sheet.
   - Settings «مركز المساعدة» navigates to `HelpScreen` with 8 expandable Q&A cards and «أرني في التطبيق» action buttons.
   - Settings «إعادة عرض التعليمات» calls `GuidePreferences.resetAll(context)` and shows a toast.

## Decisions and doubts
- Decoupled bubble placement into pure function `GuidePlacement.calculateBubblePlacement` with zero platform dependencies, allowing edge case verification (screen margins, target near bottom vs top, arrow alignment) under JUnit tests.
- Preserved `LicenseManager.kt` unchanged, adding clean migration in `GuidePreferences.isTourDone` that mirrors the legacy flag if present and clears both upon `resetAll()`.
