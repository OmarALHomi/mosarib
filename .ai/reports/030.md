# Report 030: Voice input: dictation, voice search, and spoken commands that fill a form

## Summary
Implemented native Android voice recognition via an in-app push-to-talk `SpeechRecognizer` engine and pure parsers without external AI, custom backend, or new dependencies. Includes Arabic number parsing (separators, fractions, ASCII and Eastern Arabic digits), duration parsing (digital, duals, Yemeni dialect "ونص" variants), command extraction (`SESSION`, `RECEIPT`, `EXPENSE`, `PURCHASE`, `DISCOUNT`), and customer fuzzy matching with learned aliases.

### Round 3 Implementation Details:
1. **Unprefixed Spoken Phrases & Visible Outcomes Everywhere (Blocker Fix)**:
   - **Parser inference**: Supported duration phrases without explicit session verb (e.g. `"ثلاث ساعات ونص مع احمد علي من 10 الف ومسلم 20 الف"`). Cleaned joiner `"مع"` in customer query extraction (`"مع احمد علي"` → `"احمد علي"`). Added `"مسلم"` and `"ومسلم"` to paid amount trigger keywords.
   - **Unit Test**: Added `testReviewRound3Item1OwnerPhraseCandidates` verifying the owner's exact candidate list (`["ثلاث ساعات ونص مع احمد علي من 10,000 ومسلم 20,000", …]`) resolves to `SESSION`, 210 min, rate 10000, paid 20000, and customerQuery `"احمد علي"`.
   - **Customer Not Found**: If the spoken customer is not found in database:
     - The inferred form opens immediately;
     - The spoken name is placed in the customer field (`customerInputText`);
     - Shows `VoiceCustomerChooserDialog` with closest customer suggestions plus a dedicated action: **"إضافة عميل جديد باسم «...»"** (opens quick customer add).
   - **Type Choosers for Ambiguous Voice Input**:
     - *Well owner duration ambiguity*: When a well owner is mentioned with a duration without explicit verb, displays `VoiceWellOwnerActionChooserDialog` with **"سقي على حسابه / شراء ساعات منه"**.
     - *Voucher direction ambiguity*: When an amount + customer is stated without direction verb, displays `VoiceVoucherTypeChooserDialog` with **"قبض منه / صرف له / خصم له"**.
     - *Unrecognized speech*: Shows `VoiceUnrecognizedDialog` with `"سمعت: …"`, a button to retry `"إعادة التحدث"`, and `"عرض الأمثلة"`.
   - **Debug Logging**: Added debug logs via safe `VoiceRecognitionHelper.logDebug` for parsed `VoiceCommand` and the chosen branch across home and forms.
   - **Every entry point audited**: Audited Home tile, form headers, and field dictation to guarantee zero silent paths.

2. **In-App Push-to-Talk Speech Recorder (`VoiceRecordSheet`)**:
   - Replaced the external `RecognizerIntent` activity with an in-app recorder using Android's native `SpeechRecognizer` (`createSpeechRecognizer`, `startListening`, `stopListening`), reusing the same offline/online locale resolution.
   - **Round Primary Mic Button (88dp)**: Large primary button (compact 72dp for search and field dictation).
   - **Hold-to-Talk**: Starts listening on button press, continues while held, and processes results on release.
   - **Tap Mode**: Quick tap starts continuous recording; a second tap stops and processes.
   - **Clear Arabic Hints**: Displays `"اضغط مطولاً وتكلّم، ثم أفلت"` with small `"أو اضغط للبدء ثم اضغط للإنهاء"`. While active, dynamically guides with `"تكلّم الآن... أفلت الزر عند الانتهاء"` or `"اضغط الزر للإنهاء"`.
   - **Live Text**: Shows partial speech results in real time (`EXTRA_PARTIAL_RESULTS = true`, `onPartialResults`).
   - **Sound Level Ring**: Animated pulsating ring expanding around the mic button reacting to `onRmsChanged` dB levels.
   - **Long Pause Resilience**: Configured `EXTRA_SPEECH_INPUT_COMPLETE_SILENCE_LENGTH_MILLIS` and `EXTRA_SPEECH_INPUT_POSSIBLY_COMPLETE_SILENCE_LENGTH_MILLIS` to 5000ms. If the speech engine closes prematurely during hold or tap mode (`onResults`, `onEndOfSpeech`, `onError` with `ERROR_SPEECH_TIMEOUT` / `ERROR_NO_MATCH`), it immediately restarts listening and appends newly recognized text to the accumulator until the user releases.
   - **Microphone Permission (`RECORD_AUDIO`)**:
     - Declared `<uses-permission android:name="android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO" />` in `AndroidManifest.xml`.
     - First use prompts with Arabic rationale dialog: *"يحتاج الإدخال الصوتي إلى الميكرفون لسماع كلامك. لا يُحفظ أي تسجيل."*
     - If denied, displays an informative dialog and offers a direct button to open application settings.
     - No audio recordings are stored; no data leaves the device except through Google's recognizer.
   - **Unified Across All Entry Points**: Shared `VoiceRecordSheet` powers Home quick voice, session form, vouchers form, well owner purchase form, and customer search bar.
