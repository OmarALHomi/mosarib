# Report 030: Voice input: dictation, voice search, and spoken commands that fill a form

## Summary
Implemented native Android voice recognition via `RecognizerIntent.ACTION_RECOGNIZE_SPEECH` without external AI, custom backend, or new dependencies. Created pure parsers for Arabic numbers (MSA, Yemeni dialect, separators, fractions, and raw digits in ASCII and Eastern Arabic sets), spoken durations, structured commands (`SESSION`, `RECEIPT`, `EXPENSE`, `PURCHASE`, `DISCOUNT`), and an Arabic customer matcher with persistent learned aliases.

### Round 2 Implementation Details:
1. **Google Real Output & Number Separators (Phrase 1)**:
   - Handled thousands separators in `SpokenNumberParser.cleanNumberSeparators`: comma `"10,000"`, Arabic comma `"10٬000"`, space `"10 000"`, Eastern digits `"١٠٬٠٠٠"`, and dot followed by 3 digits `"10.000"`.
   - Expanded paid words trigger regex to accept: `دفعه`, `دفعة`, `ودفع`, `و دفع`, `ودفعه`, `دافع`, `مدفوع`, `سلّم`, `سلم`, `سلمه`, `سلمة`, `وسلم`, `و سلم`, `وسلّم`, `سلمت`, `دفعنا`, `دفعت`, normalizing `ة`→`ه` and diacritics before matching.
   - For `SESSION` and `PURCHASE`, split `afterDur` into `ratePart` and `paidPart` **before** parsing the rate, so the rate text ends strictly at the paid word. Added lookahead to the total-words stripper so it never consumes trailing paid phrases.
   - Added unit test for exact phrase: `"سقي أحمد العولقي ساعتين ونص من 10,000 دفعه 5000"` -> 150 min, rate 10000, paid 5000.
2. **"ونص" and Duration Variants**:
   - Reordered `SpokenDurationParser.parseSpokenDuration` to evaluate dual and hour patterns *before* standalone fractions, fixing phrases like `"ساعتين و نص ساعه"` to resolve to 150 min instead of 30 min.
   - Added digital time notation support (`\b\d{1,2}:\d{2}\b`, e.g. `"2:30"` -> 150 min).
   - Added full support and unit tests for: `"ونص"`, `"و نص"`, `"ونصف"`, `"و نصف"`, `"ونُص"`, `"و30"`, `"و 30 دقيقه"`, `"و نص ساعه"`, `"ساعتين ونص ساعه"`, `"٢ ساعه ونص"`, `"2:30"`, `"ساعتين و نصف"`, `"الا ربع"`, `"إلا ربع"`.
3. **Show What Google Heard**:
   - Under `"فهمت: …"` in `VoiceSummaryBanner`, added a muted line `"سمعت: {rawText}"` showing the exact candidate string used.
   - Added `if (BuildConfig.DEBUG) Log.d("VoiceInput", "candidates: $candidates")` in `VoiceRecognitionHelper.parseFirstValidCommand`.
4. **Banner on Every Voice-Filled Form**:
   - Added `VoiceSummaryBanner` to `AddVoucherBottomSheet` (receipt, expense, discount) with speaker and "إعادة" buttons.
   - Prefilling forms via header mic also shows the banner dynamically across sessions, vouchers, and purchases.
5. **Recognition Support (2b)**:
   - Distinguished `canDownloadOffline` (`supportedOnDeviceLanguages` contains Arabic) from "Arabic is not offered on the device".
   - Only offer model download when `canDownloadOffline` is true. When not offered, display an informative dialog without a download button.

## Decisions and doubts
- Separator dots: Dots followed by 3 digits (e.g. `10.000`) are treated as thousands separators, while decimals with 1 or 2 digits (e.g. `2.5`) are preserved.
- Parallel work isolation: `CustomerDetailScreen.kt`, `features/settings/*`, and `SessionDetailBottomSheet.kt` were strictly untouched.
