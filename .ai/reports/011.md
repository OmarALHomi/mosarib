# Report 011: Customer detail — one place per action, compact ledger, clearer header

## Summary
Unified actions in customer detail so every action has a single clear home: header actions reserved for contact and documents ("اتصال"، "إرسال كشف"، "كشف PDF"، بالإضافة إلى "تسجيل هدر" لأصحاب الآبار)، while direct financial operations are handled by the two primary buttons under the balance card ("قبض (له)" و"صرف (عليه)" للعميل العادي، و"شراء ساعات" و"دفع له (صرف)" لصاحب البئر). The header avatar was upgraded to a 56dp circle with 2 initials and dynamic colors from `AvatarPalette`, the stats grid was made dynamic hiding irrelevant zero-value entries, and the ledger was migrated to unified `OperationCard` items grouped by day with continuous running balance computation and reconciliation against `CustomerRepository`.
