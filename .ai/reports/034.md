- **Bidi Isolation**: Wrapped Latin names (`Google Drive`, `PDF`) in Unicode isolates `\u2068...\u2069` (`⁨...⁩`) ensuring correct RTL rendering and punctuation ordering across all Android versions.
- **Accessibility & 360 dp Responsiveness**: Applied `FlowRow` for feature pills and `verticalScroll(rememberScrollState())` inside onboarding page columns to guarantee zero text truncation at 360 dp width and with 1.3x font scaling.
- **RTL Orientation**: Page indicator dots and navigation actions ("تخطي" and "التالي" with start/end arrows) align with RTL layout direction; the final page displays "ابدأ الآن" with no emojis or exclamation marks.
- **Zero Owner Data Impact**: Captured all onboarding pages and themes on the physical device via exported `androidx.compose.ui.tooling.PreviewActivity` intents, preserving existing SQLite databases and account sessions. Overridden density (320 dpi for 360 dp width) was cleanly restored to physical default (300 dpi).
