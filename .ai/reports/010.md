# Report 010: Compact customer cards and operation rows

## Summary
Designed and integrated a unified, compact `OperationRow` component across the operations list in `VouchersScreen`, replacing large multi-row cards with standardized single-row items showing type icons, titles, subtitles, formatted amounts, and kebab menus. Redesigned `CustomerCardItem` into a compact single-row card (≤ 80dp) featuring an initials-based avatar with soft colors from `AvatarPalette`, trailing balances without amount in words, and relocated actions. Tuned the customers list summary bar and padding to ensure complete visibility of the last card.
