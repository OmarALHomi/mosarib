## Long-press Menus per Operation Type
1. **Session Operations (`SessionOp`):**
   - **فتح حساب العميل** (shown when operation has a customer, navigates to customer detail)
   - **إرسال الفاتورة** (shown when customer phone is not empty, sends WhatsApp/SMS)
   - **تصدير PDF** (generates and shares irrigation invoice PDF)
   - **تعديل** (opens edit session sheet)
   - **حذف** (prompts session deletion)
2. **Voucher Operations (`VoucherOp` - Receipt, Expense, Discount):**
   - **فتح حساب العميل** (shown when voucher has an associated customer)
   - **مشاركة السند (واتساب / SMS)** (shown when customer phone is not empty)
   - **طباعة سند PDF** (generates receipt or disbursement voucher PDF)
   - **تعديل السند** (opens voucher editing sheet)
   - **حذف السند** (prompts voucher deletion)
3. **Purchase Operations (`PurchaseOp`):**
   - **فتح حساب العميل** (navigates to well owner account)
   - **مشاركة فاتورة الشراء** (shown when well owner phone is not empty)
   - **تعديل الشراء** (opens purchase editing sheet)
   - **حذف الشراء** (prompts purchase deletion)

## Decisions and doubts
- `MainScreen.kt` was kept untouched because `MosaribNavBar` encapsulates its own floating pill surface, height, and outer padding (`horizontal = 16.dp`, `bottom = 8.dp + navigationBarsPadding()`), nesting transparently inside Scaffold's `bottomBar` without requiring host layout adjustments.
- For dividers inside groups, `0.5.dp` outlineVariant dividers are drawn with start inset of `60.dp` for `OperationRow` and `64.dp` for `CustomerCardItem` under text, adapting symmetrically for RTL layout directions.
