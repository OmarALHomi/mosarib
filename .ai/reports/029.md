# Report 029: Voice reading free for everyone; say the hourly rate in the sentences

## Summary
Made speech reading (`SpeakButton`, operation cards, preview and test buttons, voice selection) completely free for all users without license checks or lock badges. Kept subscriber entitlement strictly on "إرسال كشف صوتي" via the new centralized `SpeechAccess.canSendVoiceStatement(context)`. Updated spoken sentences to state the hourly rate after the duration ("ساعتان من عشرة آلاف"), normalized "مِئَة" pronunciation with fatha on the hamza, expanded the pronunciation dictionary to ignore existing diacritics/shaddah, and added the "قراءة متصلة" switch (default ON).
