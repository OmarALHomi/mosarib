# Report 066: App re-checks its subscription when it comes back to the foreground

## Summary
MainActivity.onResume now calls `AppSyncEngine.resyncOnResume`, which runs the existing `LicenseManager.syncRemoteSubscriptionStatus` at most once per 10 minutes (pure `shouldResync`; the startup sync also stamps the time). The sync now returns `SyncChange` (NONE/ACTIVATED/EXTENDED); a change is emitted on `AppSyncEngine.licenseChanged`, and MainScreen refreshes activation and shows the toast «تم تفعيل اشتراكك 🎉» or «تم تمديد اشتراكك حتى <date>».
